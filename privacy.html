Privacy Policy

NotchWell: File Shelf & Music · Last updated November 13, 2026

NotchWell doesn't collect any data. Everything you use it for stays on your Mac. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Who I am

NotchWell: File Shelf & Music ("NotchWell" or "the app") is made by Muhammad Fahid Khan, an independent developer based in Pakistan. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information collected

None. The app doesn't collect, send, sell or share personal data. I never see your notes, files, clipboard, calendar, music or anything else you use it with.

The app does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in
  • use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools
  • use cookies

Where your data is stored

Your settings, notes, shelf files and app shortcuts are stored only on your Mac, inside NotchWell's own app folder. Clipboard history is kept in memory only and is cleared when NotchWell quits. Photos and videos you take go to the shelf, and to your Pictures and Movies folders only if you turn that on.

Permissions

NotchWell only asks for access when you use a feature that needs it. Everything it reads stays on your Mac.

  • Music and Spotify: to show and control what's playing.
  • Location: to show the weather where you are.
  • Calendars and Reminders: to show your next events and today's reminders.
  • Camera and Microphone: for the mirror, photos and videos. The camera only runs while the Mirror tab is open or a video is recording.
  • Bluetooth: to show a short alert when a device connects, if you turn that on.
  • Downloads folder: to show download progress, if you turn that on.

You can change these anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Apple services

To show the weather, NotchWell sends your approximate location to Apple's WeatherKit service. To find album art for songs streamed from Apple Music, it sends the song title and artist to Apple's public iTunes Search service. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy. I don't receive or keep any of this information.

Deleting your data

Deleting NotchWell removes its data from your Mac. You can also clear the shelf and clipboard history at any time in Settings.

Contacting me

If you email me, I'll use your email address only to reply.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
