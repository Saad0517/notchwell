Common questions

How do I open NotchWell?

Move the pointer to the notch at the top of your screen, or click it. Move the pointer away and it closes. You can also swipe down on the notch with two fingers to open it, and swipe up to close it.

Where are the settings?

Click the NotchWell icon in the menu bar and choose Settings, or click the gear in the open notch. NotchWell has no Dock icon.

My Mac doesn't have a notch.

NotchWell still works. It shows a small notch-shaped bar at the top center of the screen. In Settings → General you can choose which display it appears on.

Now Playing doesn't show my music.

NotchWell works with Apple Music and Spotify. The first time music plays, macOS asks to let NotchWell control the app. If you said no, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation → NotchWell and turn on Music and Spotify.

Weather, calendar or reminders are missing.

Open the Today tab and tap Allow Access on the card. If you turned access off before, turn it back on in System Settings → Privacy & Security under Location Services, Calendars or Reminders.

Where are my notes and shelf files stored?

Only on your Mac. NotchWell has no accounts and no servers, so I can't see or recover them. Deleting NotchWell also deletes its data.

How do I see the welcome tour again?

Click the NotchWell icon in the menu bar and choose Welcome Tour.

How do I stop NotchWell from opening at login?

Turn off Launch at login in Settings → General. To quit, click the menu bar icon and choose Quit NotchWell.
